# Fees

## Monthly account fees
Loretta Bank cheque and savings accounts have no monthly account fee.

## Transaction fees
- Transfers between your own Loretta Bank accounts are free.
- Payments to other Loretta Bank accounts are free.
- Cash withdrawals at a branch are free. ATM withdrawals at partner ATMs cost R10 per withdrawal.
- Cash deposits at a branch are free.

## Statements
Downloading statements in the app is free. Stamped statements requested from a branch cost R50 each.
