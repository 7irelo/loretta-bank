# Privacy and your data

## What we keep
We keep the information needed to run your accounts: identity details, contact details,
transactions and an audit log of account activity, as required by South African law (POPIA and
FICA).

## AI features
AI features are optional and can be switched off in Settings. When they are on, the assistant can
read your accounts and transactions only, with the same permissions you have in the app. It can
prepare payments or card freezes for you to confirm, but it cannot complete them on its own.
Personal identifiers are masked before any text is sent to an AI provider, and providers may not
use your data to train models.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete data we are not
legally required to keep. Contact support to make a request.
