# Payments and limits

## Who you can pay
You can transfer money between your own accounts, or pay any Loretta Bank account using its
account number. Account numbers start with LOR followed by ten digits.

## Limits
- The maximum for a single payment or withdrawal is R50 000.
- The total you can pay out or withdraw in one day is R100 000. The day resets at midnight
  South African time.
- Limits apply to transfers and withdrawals. Money you receive has no limit.

## Before you can move money
Your identity must be verified (KYC) before you can open accounts, pay anyone or withdraw cash.
Verification usually takes one business day after you upload your ID document and proof of
address.

## Payment status
A payment is normally completed within seconds. If our ledger is temporarily unavailable, the
payment shows as Pending and completes automatically once the ledger is back. You are never
charged twice: every payment has a unique key that prevents duplicates.

## Reversals
Completed payments to another person cannot be reversed by you. If you paid the wrong account,
contact support immediately; we will contact the recipient but cannot guarantee recovery.
