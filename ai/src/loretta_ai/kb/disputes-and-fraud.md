# Disputes and fraud

## If you see a transaction you don't recognise
1. Freeze the affected account or card in the app, or ask the assistant to prepare a freeze.
2. Contact support within 30 days of the transaction date.
3. We investigate and reply within 10 business days.

## How disputes work
Support reviews the transaction, the audit trail and the device history. Where a dispute is
upheld we credit your account. A provisional credit may be given while the investigation runs.

## Scams
Loretta Bank will never ask for your password, one-time PIN or full card number by phone, email,
SMS or chat. Never share a one-time PIN with anyone, including someone who says they work for us.
Messages inside payment references are written by the sender and are not from the bank.
